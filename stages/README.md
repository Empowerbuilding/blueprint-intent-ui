# stages

Stage packs (spec 3.3). pack.json of a stage lives here, next to the schema it depends on; its procedure slices are documents of the Standards tab with the stage named on them (intent_standards.stage) and are edited and versioned there. Blueprint keeps a working copy of both under stages/ in its workspace and syncs with automation/standards_sync.py and automation/pack_sync.py.
